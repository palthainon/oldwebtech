# PMP source inventory (reviewed 2026-10-12)

## Current exam blueprint

PMI says the revised PMP exam launched in July 2026. Its current [Examination Content Outline](https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/new-pmp-examination-content-outline-2026.pdf?rev=eb2fb918d0f94ccebb8bc1587021f60c) assigns **People 33%**, **Process 41%**, and **Business Environment 26%**. About **40%** of items use predictive approaches; the remaining **60%** are divided between adaptive/agile and hybrid approaches. PMI explicitly emphasizes scenario-based application, AI, sustainability, stakeholder engagement, outcomes, value, and business impact. The outline describes case/scenario, graphic-based, matching, multiple-response, and other item types. Use the July 2026 outline as the authority for objective tags and sampling weights; do not infer coverage solely from a textbook or PMBOK edition.

## Original question topics to build

1. **People (33%)**: shared vision; conflict resolution; team leadership and role clarity; stakeholder identification, engagement, and expectation alignment; knowledge transfer; communication strategy, reporting, and feedback loops.
2. **Process (41%)**: choosing predictive, adaptive, or hybrid delivery; integrated plans and dependencies; scope and backlog choices; incremental value and benefits measures; resources, procurement and contracts, finance and reserves, quality and sustainability, schedule variance, status artifacts, and transition/closure.
3. **Business Environment (26%)**: governance and escalation; compliance including security and sustainability; change control; impediments and issues; risk response; continuous improvement; organizational change; regulatory, technology, geopolitical, and market changes.
4. **Cross-cutting item design**: create realistic decisions with plausible alternatives and explanations for every option. For a 300-question pilot, the domain weights imply **99 People, 123 Process, and 78 Business Environment** items. Vary predictive, adaptive/agile, and hybrid contexts across *each* domain; the blueprint's 40/60 figure is approximate and does not specify the adaptive-versus-hybrid split.

BCcampus is strong background for the fundamentals: project initiation, stakeholders, scope, schedule, resource and budget planning, procurement, quality, communications, risk, implementation, and closure. It predates the 2026 exam, so do not rely on it for the new emphasis on AI, sustainability, modern agile/hybrid delivery, and value measurement. Use the current PMI outline and current, independently verified sources to fill those gaps.

## Sources located but not copied

- [PMI PMBOK Guide](https://www.pmi.org/standards/pmbok): publisher standard, not an open-license textbook. PMI says it is free for members; that does not grant republication rights for a public question service.
- [PMI Agile Practice Guide, Second Edition](https://www.pmi.org/standards/agile): July 2026 publisher guide covering adaptive delivery, AI-enabled work, sustainability, and value. PMI identifies digital member access, not an open license. Link as optional user study material; independently author explanations.

Avoid using PMI's sample exam questions or paid prep-bank questions as generation seed text. The public outline is sufficient to map objectives; the openly licensed textbook can provide conceptual background with attribution where actual text is reused.
