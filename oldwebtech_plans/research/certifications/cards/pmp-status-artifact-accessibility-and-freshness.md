## Behavior

- When evaluating project status, tailor the artifacts to the reporting need, keep them reviewed and updated, and make them accessible to the people who use them. Reconcile the current metrics with actual progress before communicating status.

## Gotchas

- A polished status view can still be misleading if its underlying artifacts are stale or inaccessible. Reassess whether artifact management is working as the project changes.

- Check PMI's July 2026 Process Task 9 enablers for metrics, artifacts, progress, communication, and continuing artifact-management review. This card describes the outline's expected practice; no live project was assessed.
