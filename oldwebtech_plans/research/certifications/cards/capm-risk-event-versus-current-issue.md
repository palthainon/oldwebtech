## Behavior

- Classify an uncertain future event that could affect a project objective as a risk, and record its assessment and planned response in the risk register. Once a condition is present, treat it as an issue to record and monitor in the issue log, with an action for its current effect.

## Defaults or baseline

- PMI's 2026 Lexicon defines risk by uncertainty and possible positive or negative effect. It defines an issue as a current condition that may affect objectives; actual damage is not required before the condition can be logged as an issue.

## Gotchas

- Leaving a materialized risk only in the risk register can defer action while the team keeps discussing probability. The current condition needs issue handling; the related risk record can preserve how it was anticipated.

## Verify

- Locate the CAPM Domain 1 Task 1 enabler distinguishing issues and risks, then compare the 2026 PMI Lexicon entries for risk, issue, risk register, and issue log. PMI's *Risks vs. issues* practitioner article describes moving a materialized risk to an issue log while retaining its risk history.
