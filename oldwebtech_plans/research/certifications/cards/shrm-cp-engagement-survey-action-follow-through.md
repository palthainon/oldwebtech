## Behavior

- After interpreting an employee engagement survey, identify an issue the organization can address, communicate the findings and planned response, and later report what changed. Use follow-up discussion when the result needs context before choosing an action.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2026 SHRM BASK maps survey design, administration, analysis, and interpretation to all HR professionals. It separately describes communicating a survey action plan and results among advanced HR proficiency indicators.

## Gotchas

- Publishing a survey score or launching a workplace change without connecting that change to employee feedback leaves employees unable to see how their input was used. An overall score can also conceal a problem concentrated in one team; interpret the relevant result before selecting an intervention.

## Verify

- In the 2026 SHRM BASK, review Employee Engagement & Retention key concepts and proficiency indicators on printed pages 59–60. Compare SHRM's discussion of survey metrics, focus groups, result communication, and progress recaps. In a sample scenario, name the issue, a feasible response, the audience for the result, and a later progress update. A qualified HR reviewer should assess any future situational question.
