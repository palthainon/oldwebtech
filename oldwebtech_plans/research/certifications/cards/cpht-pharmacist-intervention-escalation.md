## Behavior

- When processing identifies an issue that requires pharmacist intervention, route it to the pharmacist through the pharmacy's workflow. PTCE examples include drug utilization review, adverse drug events, OTC recommendations, therapeutic substitutions, misuse, adherence, post-immunization delivery care, allergies, and drug interactions.
- Follow the pharmacy's approved workflow and applicable state technician scope requirements.

## Defaults or baseline

- The PTCE knowledge reference identifies the listed concerns as issues requiring pharmacist intervention.

## Gotchas

- The PTCE examples identify intervention topics; they do not replace the pharmacy's escalation procedure or state scope requirements.
- Technician scope and workflow requirements vary by state; this card does not establish a universal scope rule.

## Verify

- Confirm the issue against the current pharmacy escalation workflow and relevant state requirements, then obtain pharmacist intervention as required by that workflow.

## Applies to

- PTCE 2026 Patient Safety and Quality Assurance, objective 3.3.
