## Behavior

- When determining whether a project or product is ready for delivery, use the requirements traceability matrix or product backlog.
- Define acceptance criteria appropriate to the situation as part of validating requirements through product delivery.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2023 CAPM Examination Content Outline names a requirements traceability matrix or product backlog under requirements gathering, and names either artifact as the basis for a delivery-readiness determination.

## Gotchas

- The outline presents defining acceptance criteria and determining readiness from the traceability matrix or backlog as separate enablers under Task 6.

## Verify

- Define the applicable acceptance criteria, then determine readiness from the requirements traceability matrix or product backlog.

## Applies to

- CAPM Domain 4, Task 3 (requirements traceability matrix/product backlog) and Task 6 (validate requirements through product delivery).
