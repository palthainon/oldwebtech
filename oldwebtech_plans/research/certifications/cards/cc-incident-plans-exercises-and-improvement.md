## Behavior

- An incident response plan should be exercised so responders can test how policies and procedures work in practice. Use exercise findings and incident reviews to identify improvements to the response program and related risk management plans.

## Defaults or baseline

- NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 treats incident response as part of broader cybersecurity risk management, rather than a standalone set of steps used only after an incident begins.

## Gotchas

- A plan that exists only as a document can fail when roles, communications, or data handling steps are unclear in practice; an exercise can expose those gaps before a real incident.

## Verify

- Review ISC2 CC outline objective 5.3, then NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 sections 2–3 and its CSF 2.0 community profile recommendations on incident response plans, exercises, and improvement.

## Applies to

- ISC2 CC Domain 5, objective 5.3: understand IR plan implementation and incident response exercises.
