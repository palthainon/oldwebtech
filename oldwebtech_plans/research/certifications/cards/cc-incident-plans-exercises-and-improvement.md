## Behavior

- Exercise an incident response plan through testing or tabletop exercises. Use findings from exercises and incident reviews to identify improvements to the incident response program and cybersecurity risk management efforts.

## Defaults or baseline

- NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 treats incident response as part of broader cybersecurity risk management, rather than a standalone set of steps used only after an incident begins.

## Gotchas

- Exercises and tests can help evaluate the program and prepare staff and involved third parties for future incident response activities.

## Verify

- Review ISC2 CC outline objective 5.3, then NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, section 3, CSF 2.0 community-profile elements ID.IM-02 and ID.IM-04.

## Applies to

- ISC2 CC Domain 5, objective 5.3: understand IR plan implementation and incident response exercises.
