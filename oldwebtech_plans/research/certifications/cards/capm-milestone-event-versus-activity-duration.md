## Behavior

- Represent a significant checkpoint or event (such as completion of a deliverable) as a milestone; estimate duration for the scheduled work activity that produces or supports that checkpoint. When asked to distinguish the two, identify the milestone as an event in the schedule and duration as the time estimated for an activity.

## Defaults or baseline

- PMI's scheduling standard defines a schedule milestone as a significant event with zero duration. It defines a schedule activity as a discrete component of work that normally has an estimated duration, cost, and resource requirements.

## Gotchas

- A milestone's planned date can move when schedule logic or progress changes; that does not turn it into a duration-bearing work activity. Treating the checkpoint itself as elapsed work time can confuse progress and remaining-work estimates. The last sentence is a scheduling inference from PMI's event and activity definitions.
