## Behavior

- When a U.S. federal negotiated acquisition uses the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) tradeoff process, evaluate the stated non-price factors and price. Use only the factors and relative importance stated in the solicitation.

## Defaults or baseline

- When requirements are clearly definable and the risk of unsuccessful performance is minimal, price can have a dominant role in source selection. In a FAR lowest-price technically acceptable selection, award goes to the lowest evaluated price that meets the stated acceptability standards. Tradeoffs are not permitted.

## Gotchas

- Do not select the lowest price in a FAR tradeoff acquisition without applying the stated non-price factors. A higher-priced proposal must provide benefits that merit its additional cost. Document the tradeoff rationale. The FAR applies to U.S. federal acquisitions. The solicitation and the applicable procurement authority govern an actual award.
