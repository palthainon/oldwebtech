## Behavior

- When a project solicits competing technical approaches through an RFP, evaluate the proposed approach and its project value along with price. Include reviewers who can assess the technical options.

## Defaults or baseline

- A request for quote (RFQ) fits a well-defined good or service available from several sources and focuses heavily on price after quality and schedule requirements are met. An RFP can compare proposed solutions against quality, schedule, and technical needs as well as price.

## Gotchas

- Choosing the lowest RFP price without checking technical fit can miss the proposal that offers the best project value. Do not treat the RFQ and RFP selection pattern in a 2014 textbook as a universal procurement rule; the solicitation's criteria and applicable organizational or jurisdictional rules govern an actual award.
