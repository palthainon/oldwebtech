## Behavior

- When stakeholders jump to a preferred solution, first establish the project’s boundaries and clarify the business need the work is meant to address. Then develop requirements that explain what is needed before choosing how to meet that need. This keeps the solution discussion anchored to an agreed scope and intended outcome.

## Defaults or baseline

- Treat stakeholder agreement on project scope as an explicit planning outcome. The July 2026 ECO includes obtaining stakeholder agreement and breaking down scope under Process Task 2.

## Gotchas

- Starting with a favored technical solution can leave the underlying need unclear. PMI’s requirements paper describes this sequence as a source of solutions that fail to address organizational needs; scope boundaries and collaborative requirements work help surface that mismatch before detailed solution choices.

## Verify

- In a scenario where a sponsor or team proposes a solution before agreeing what problem the project must solve, identify the missing step: work with stakeholders to define the project boundaries and clarify the need before decomposing requirements into a solution. Do not infer a universal approval method or a mandatory scope artifact from this card.
