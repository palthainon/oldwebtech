## Behavior

- When two team members assume the other owns a decision or work item, clarify the expected outcome and decision boundary with the team, then make responsibilities explicit. Invite the affected members to shape how they will coordinate so the agreed ownership uses their relevant experience and skills.

## Gotchas

- Assigning work by job title alone can leave a decision owner unclear when responsibilities overlap. Unresolved ownership can lead to duplicated effort or a decision that nobody makes; treat this as a scenario-based risk to check, not a rule that every role overlap causes failure.

## Verify

- In a scenario with overlapping responsibilities, check whether the response establishes team expectations, clarifies responsibility, and includes the affected team members in a way that uses their skills and perspectives. Confirm the fit against the July 2026 PMP People Task 3 enablers and the team-norm and ownership guidance in PMI's team report. No live team was assessed.
