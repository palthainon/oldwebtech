## Behavior

Use a Y-axis constant line to display a fixed numeric threshold on a supported chart.

## Defaults or baseline

A Y-axis constant line is horizontal at a fixed value. An Average line uses the average of a measure. A clustered column chart supports both.

## Gotchas

An Average line does not represent a fixed operational threshold unless the computed average happens to match it.
