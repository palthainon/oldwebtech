## Behavior

- Work with business partners to identify the current HR challenge or opportunity before proposing a solution.
- Design an HR response with stakeholders, define its goals and outcomes, and use those goals to guide the solution.

## Defaults or baseline

- Consultation includes evaluating business challenges and designing HR solutions that meet business needs.

## Gotchas

- A solution without clear goals and outcomes is harder to design against the business need or evaluate after implementation.

## Verify

- Check that stakeholders helped identify the need and that the solution has clear goals and outcomes tied to it.

## Applies to

- SHRM-CP 2026 BASK, Consultation competency and related proficiency indicators for all HR professionals.
