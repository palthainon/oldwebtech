## Behavior

- Work with business partners and leaders to identify current or future HR challenges and opportunities for HR solutions.
- Partner with stakeholders to suggest HR solutions, define clear goals and outcomes, and use those goals and outcomes to drive solution design.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2026 BASK defines Consultation as working with organizational stakeholders to evaluate business challenges and identify opportunities for change initiatives and HR solutions that meet changing customer and business needs.

## Gotchas

- Clear goals and outcomes are an all-HR-professional proficiency indicator for designing HR solutions.

## Verify

- Check that stakeholders helped identify the need and that the solution has clear goals and outcomes tied to it.

## Applies to

- SHRM-CP 2026 BASK, Consultation competency and related proficiency indicators for all HR professionals.
