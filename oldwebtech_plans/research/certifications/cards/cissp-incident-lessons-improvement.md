# Behavior

- As recovery efforts conclude, review what happened, what actions were taken, and how effective they were with the parties involved.
- Use the review to identify and prioritize improvements to incident response and broader cybersecurity risk management.

# Gotchas

- Closing an incident after service restoration without capturing lessons can leave response weaknesses in place for the next event.

# Verify

- Review the incident follow-up or lessons-learned record and confirm it identifies actions, effectiveness, participants, and prioritized improvements.

# Applies to

- CISSP Domain 7, objective 7.6 (incident management, including lessons learned). No questions linked yet.
