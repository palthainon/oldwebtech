## Behavior

After enabling personalization for a report, turn Personalize visual off for an individual visual to exclude that visual.

## Defaults or baseline

Report enablement allows personalization across the report. Authors can then control it per page or per visual.

## Gotchas

A report-wide switch is broader than a change for one visual.

## Verify

Read the Microsoft sections below. No live Power BI report was tested.
