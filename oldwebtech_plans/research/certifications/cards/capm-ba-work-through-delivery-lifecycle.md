## Behavior

- When a team chooses or changes its delivery approach, tailor the business analysis work to that approach while continuing to discover needs, validate the delivered product, and assess actual results across the product life cycle. Business analysis is useful in adaptive and traditional delivery; its activities are not exhausted by the first requirements document.

## Defaults or baseline

- PMI's CAPM outline asks candidates to determine the business analysis role in adaptive and predictive approaches. The specific analysis techniques and their timing should fit the project's context and delivery method.

## Gotchas

- Treating business analysis as a one-time requirements handoff can leave later delivery cycles without validation of the product's results or input for corrections and enhancements.

## Verify

- For a proposed delivery approach, identify when the team will revisit stakeholder needs, validate the product during delivery, and assess actual outcomes after a delivery cycle. Check those activities against the approach rather than assuming the same up-front document ends all analysis work.

## Applies to

- CAPM Domain 4, Task 5: determine how project methodologies influence business analysis processes, including the business analysis role in adaptive or predictive approaches.
