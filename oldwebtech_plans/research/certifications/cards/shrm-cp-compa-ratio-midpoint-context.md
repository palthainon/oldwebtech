## Behavior

- To describe an employee's position relative to the midpoint of the assigned pay range, divide current salary by that range's midpoint and express the result as a percentage. A result below 100% means pay is below that midpoint; review the organization's range, peer pay, and compensation philosophy before using the number in a pay decision.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2026 SHRM BASK includes compa-ratio among Total Rewards metrics and calls for interpreting compensation data from several sources. SHRM's glossary defines the metric as current pay relative to a range midpoint; it links the midpoint to market pay only when that range was set to represent full market pay.

## Gotchas

- A low compa-ratio alone does not establish that pay is inequitable or that a particular increase is required. The midpoint is a range reference, and internal peer comparisons can reveal context that a single midpoint ratio omits.

## Verify

- In the SHRM BASK, locate Total Rewards metrics and its data-interpretation proficiency indicator. Compare the HR Glossary's Compa-Ratio entry with SHRM Labs' section on existing employee compensation data. For a sample salary and midpoint, calculate the ratio, then identify what peer and range context is still missing. A qualified compensation or HR reviewer should assess any future situational pay recommendation or legal claim.
