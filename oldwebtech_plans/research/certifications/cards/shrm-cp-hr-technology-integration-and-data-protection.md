## Behavior

- When implementing HR technology that handles candidate or employee information, include both enterprise-system integration and workforce-data protection in the implementation decision.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2026 BASK lists integration with other enterprise systems and protecting workforce data as Technology Management proficiency indicators for all HR professionals.

## Gotchas

- A tool that improves one HR workflow can still miss this competency when its implementation leaves the enterprise integration or workforce-data protection decision unresolved.

## Verify

- Review the 2026 BASK Technology Management section, printed pages 72–73, especially the indicators for all HR professionals. For a scenario, identify the HR service and workforce data involved, then check both implementation concerns. Do not infer a specific law, security control, or vendor obligation from this card.

## Applies to

- SHRM-CP 2026 BASK, Organization Knowledge Domain, Technology Management. No question IDs. Qualified HR review is required before situational question writing.
