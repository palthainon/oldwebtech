## Behavior

- When implementing technology for HR services or candidate and employee data, select a solution that integrates with other enterprise systems. Use the technology in a manner that protects workforce data.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2026 BASK lists integration with other enterprise systems and protection of workforce data as Technology Management proficiency indicators for all HR professionals.

## Gotchas

- An integration success does not show that workforce data is protected. Check both indicators when an HR technology decision affects candidate or employee data.

## Verify

- Review the 2026 BASK Technology Management section on printed and PDF pages 73–74, especially the indicators for all HR professionals. For a scenario, identify the HR service, candidate or employee data, and enterprise-system integration. Then check that the intended technology use protects workforce data. Use the applicable organization privacy and security rules for a control decision. Do not infer a specific law, security control, or vendor obligation from this card.

## Applies to

- SHRM-CP 2026 BASK, Organization Knowledge Domain, Technology Management. No question IDs. Qualified HR review is required before situational question writing.
