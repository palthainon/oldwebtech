## Behavior

- When a near miss, medication error, adverse effect, or product-integrity concern falls under the pharmacy's event-reporting procedure, use that procedure to report it. Reports can inform later investigation and quality improvement; the local process determines who reports, when, and where.

## Defaults or baseline

- The PTCB objective includes near misses, medication errors, adverse effects, product integrity, root-cause analysis, and continuous quality improvement under event-reporting procedures. Follow the applicable pharmacy process for how and where to report.

## Gotchas

- A near miss is included in the PTCE event-reporting topic. In AHRQ's Common Formats classification, it is an event that did not reach a patient, so do not assume the topic is limited to errors that caused harm.

## Verify

- Check the current pharmacy event-reporting procedure for the reportable events, reporting channel, and required details. Have a pharmacist or qualified pharmacy technician confirm the local workflow before using this card operationally.

## Applies to

- PTCE 2026 Patient Safety and Quality Assurance, objective 3.4.
