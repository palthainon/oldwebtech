## Behavior

- When a reportable near miss, medication error, adverse effect, or product-integrity concern occurs, follow the pharmacy event-reporting procedure. A report can support later investigation and quality improvement. The local procedure determines the reporter, time, channel, and required details.

## Defaults or baseline

- PTCE objective 3.4 lists event-reporting procedures for medication errors, adverse effects, product integrity, MedWatch, VAERS, near misses, root-cause analysis, and continuous quality improvement. Follow the applicable pharmacy procedure for how and where to report.

## Gotchas

- A near miss is included in the PTCE event-reporting topic. In AHRQ Common Formats, a near miss did not reach a patient. An event that reached a patient is an incident, even if no harm occurred.

## Verify

- Check the current pharmacy event-reporting procedure for reportable events, reporting channel, required details, and escalation steps. Have a pharmacist or qualified pharmacy technician confirm the local workflow before operational use.

## Applies to

- PTCE 2026 Patient Safety and Quality Assurance, objective 3.4.
