## Behavior

- When an organization is improving performance appraisal, establish clear goals and provide feedback frequently instead of relying only on a single end-of-cycle conversation.

## Defaults or baseline

- Performance management systems can include recorded goals, and effective appraisals include goal setting and frequent feedback.

## Gotchas

- Treating appraisal as a once-a-year rating misses the ongoing goal-setting and feedback practices identified in the current SHRM BASK.

## Verify

- In the SHRM 2026 BASK, check the Employee Engagement & Retention key concepts on performance management systems and effective performance appraisal.
- Apply qualified HR review before turning this source-level principle into situational judgment questions.
