## Behavior

- Before choosing a course for an employee, compare the employee's current skills or knowledge with what the role requires. Use the identified gap to choose a relevant learning or development activity.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2026 SHRM BASK lists needs analysis within Learning & Development and gives person and organizational analysis as examples. A training needs assessment can use employee and supervisor input to identify an individual's gap.

## Gotchas

- Assigning the same course to everyone without identifying individual gaps can spend training effort where it is not needed. An individual training needs assessment also does not, by itself, provide the organization-wide competency picture needed for workforce investment decisions.

## Verify

- For a single role, name the required capability, the employee's present capability, and the gap. Check that the proposed learning activity targets that gap. If the decision is about workforce-wide capability or budget allocation, use evidence gathered across the relevant population rather than treating one employee's quick assessment as an organizational inventory.

## Applies to

- SHRM-CP 2026 BASK, People Knowledge Domain, Learning & Development. No question IDs assigned. Have a qualified HR reviewer assess future situational questions.
