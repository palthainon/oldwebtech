## Behavior

- At project setup, make decision rights and escalation thresholds clear. When a proposed decision stays within the project manager's assigned authority and tolerances, decide and report it through the agreed governance process. When it exceeds those limits or requires a trade-off reserved to the sponsor or governance body, send it to that decision owner with the relevant impact and options.

## Gotchas

- Treating the escalation path as a list of contacts without specifying what crosses each threshold leaves the team guessing about authority. Escalating every routine decision can also delay work; route decisions according to the agreed threshold and decision rights.

## Verify

- In a scenario with a decision that affects cost, scope, risk, or expected value, identify the established decision owner and threshold. Check whether the project manager acts within delegated authority or escalates the decision that exceeds it, then follows the applicable reporting path. The scenario is an application of the sources, not an example supplied by PMI.
