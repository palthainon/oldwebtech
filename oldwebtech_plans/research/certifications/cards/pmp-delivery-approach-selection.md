# Behavior

- Assess the project's needs, complexity, and magnitude before recommending predictive, adaptive, or hybrid delivery.
- The recommended approach feeds the integrated project management plan and execution strategy.

# Gotchas

- Choosing an approach solely because the organization uses it elsewhere skips the project's needs assessment. The resulting plan may fit that convention better than the project's actual complexity.
