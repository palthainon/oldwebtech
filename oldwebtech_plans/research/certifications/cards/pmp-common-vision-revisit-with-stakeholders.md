## Behavior

- When stakeholders or team members interpret the project's intended outcome differently, revisit the common vision with key stakeholders and make the purpose and intended result clear to the team. Promote that shared direction during delivery and check whether it still fits when objectives or circumstances change.

## Defaults or baseline

- A project vision describes the overall purpose and direction; project objectives make that direction more specific and may change more often than the vision.

## Gotchas

- Treating the vision as a launch statement that never needs discussion can leave different groups pursuing individually reasonable objectives without the same understanding of the project's purpose.

## Verify

- For a proposed priority or objective change, ask key stakeholders to state the outcome and purpose they expect. Compare their descriptions, resolve a material difference, and check that the team can explain how its current work supports the shared direction. This card reflects documented PMI guidance; no live project review was performed.
