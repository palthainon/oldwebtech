## Behavior

- Before choosing a staffing or development response to a future capability need, map the workforce's current roles and skills, forecast the needed capabilities, and identify the gap between them. Use that gap to compare responses such as upskilling, reskilling, or hiring.

## Defaults or baseline

- The 2026 SHRM BASK places workforce planning and organizational gap analysis within Workforce Management. Its proficiency indicators ask HR professionals to assess needed competencies and find gaps or misaligned staffing levels.

## Gotchas

- Treating a vacancy count as a full competency gap can send the organization directly to hiring even when its existing workforce has skills that could be developed or redeployed. The workforce plan needs evidence of present capabilities and future needs before selecting a response.

## Verify

- Check SHRM's 2026 BASK Workforce Management definition, key concepts, and proficiency indicators. Then follow SHRM's 2026 workforce planning roadmap steps 1–3: audit current roles and skills, forecast future needs, and compare the two to identify skills gaps. Have a qualified HR professional review future situational questions before release.
