## Behavior

- When a business analyst needs stakeholders to review requirements, choose the communication format and forum for each audience's information and approval needs. Coordinate the requirements communication schedule with the project manager's wider project communication plan.

## Defaults or baseline

- The CAPM outline expects a candidate to recommend a suitable stakeholder communication channel or tool and explain the business analyst's communication across teams. A report, presentation, or meeting is a choice to make for the audience and purpose, not a universal default.

## Gotchas

- Sending the same detailed requirements document to every audience can fail to meet different information needs. Planning business analysis communications separately from project communications can create conflicting plans.

## Verify

- For a requirements decision, identify who provides input, who must review or approve, the information each needs, the format and timing, and where the project manager incorporates that plan into overall project communications. Check the selected channel against those needs.

## Applies to

- CAPM Domain 4, Task 2: stakeholder communication and suitable channels or tools for business analysis across teams.
