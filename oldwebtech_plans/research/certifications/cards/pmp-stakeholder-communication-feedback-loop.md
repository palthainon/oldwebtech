## Behavior

- When sponsors or stakeholders need project updates, identify their reporting needs, define a communication strategy, and shape reports to those expectations. Include a way for recipients to respond so the project team can check understanding and act on feedback.

## Defaults or baseline

- A report sent or posted is a delivery of information; it does not by itself establish that the stakeholder read, understood, or acted on it. Use a feedback loop when communication effectiveness or stakeholder response matters.

## Gotchas

- Treating a generic status report as proof of alignment can leave questions or misunderstandings undiscovered. Tailor the report to the intended audience and follow up for feedback on decisions, concerns, or unclear information.

## Verify

- For a scenario about sponsor or stakeholder reporting, check that the communication approach reflects audience requirements, includes the reporting and governance needs, and provides a feedback path. Compare with PMI's current People Task 8 and the feedback-loop discussion in the cited PMI article. No live project communication was tested for this source review.
