# Behavior

- For a major software deliverable, build recurring delivery reviews and a readiness review before customer acceptance into the quality plan. Compare the deliverable with the agreed requirements, document review actions, and close them before treating the deliverable as complete.
- Use the recurring reviews to surface whether delivery is tracking to the plan and customer expectations; carry lessons from reviews into improvement.

# Defaults or baseline

- In the cited software-program example, a detailed readiness review is especially useful for a large or complex deliverable, a major handoff, or a system going into production.

# Gotchas

- Treating customer acceptance as the first quality checkpoint can reveal gaps only at handoff, leaving less time to resolve them. For a large or complex software deliverable, use the planned readiness review before it enters acceptance.

# Verify

- In a scenario involving a major software release or handoff, check for scheduled delivery reviews, a requirements-based readiness check before acceptance, and documented review actions resolved before completion.

# Applies to

- PMP Process Task 7 (plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables). No questions linked yet.
