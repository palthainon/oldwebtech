## Behavior

- Establish the position's requirements, then choose hiring methods, assessments, and scoring that evaluate the candidate's relevant skills, positional fit, and alignment with the organization's competency needs.

## Defaults or baseline

- The assessment should be selected in relation to the job requirements; the BASK lists ability and job-knowledge tests, interviews, assessment centers, and AI-based skill or competency matching among possible assessment methods.

## Gotchas

- Reusing a familiar test or scoring rule without checking it against the position's requirements can produce a score that does not answer whether the candidate is qualified for that role.

## Verify

- Identify the position requirements first. For each assessment and scoring method, state which requirement it evaluates and how the result informs the candidate decision. Have a qualified HR reviewer assess any future situational questions built from this card.

- SHRM-CP 2026 BASK, People Knowledge Domain, Talent Acquisition. No question IDs assigned. Qualified HR review is needed before situational questions are drafted or published.
