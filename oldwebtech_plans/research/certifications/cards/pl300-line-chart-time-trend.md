## Behavior

Use a line chart when the report must show the shape of a measure over time.

## Defaults or baseline

Microsoft describes continuous X-axis data as the best fit for line charts.

## Gotchas

A part-to-whole chart answers a different question. A donut shows category shares, not the shape of a time trend.

## Verify

Read Charts for comparison and trends > Line charts; Charts for part-to-whole relationships > Pie and donut charts in the linked Microsoft source. No live Power BI model was tested.
