## Behavior

- For high-alert/risk and look-alike/sound-alike medications, use the pharmacy's applicable error-prevention workflow. PTCE examples include separating inventory, Tall Man lettering, bar code use, and limiting error-prone abbreviations.
- During processing, match the prescription or medication order to the correct patient.

## Defaults or baseline

- PTCB lists these as examples of error-prevention strategies; local procedures determine how the controls are applied in a particular pharmacy.

## Gotchas

- The outline gives examples of error-prevention strategies; local procedure determines which controls apply at a processing step.

## Verify

- Review the pharmacy's current workflow for high-alert/risk and look-alike/sound-alike medications and identify the controls used at the relevant processing step.

## Applies to

- PTCE 2026 Patient Safety and Quality Assurance, objectives 3.1 and 3.2.
