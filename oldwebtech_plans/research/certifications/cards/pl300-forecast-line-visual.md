## Behavior

Use Forecast in the Analytics pane of a line chart to estimate future values from historical time data.

## Defaults or baseline

Forecast settings include Forecast length and Confidence interval.

## Gotchas

A constant reference line displays a fixed value. Anomaly detection identifies unexpected historical spikes or dips. Neither substitutes for a future estimate.
