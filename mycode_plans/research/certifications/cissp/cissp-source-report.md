## Editorial guidance

1. Draft scenario questions from underlying concepts, cite the relevant PDF section and exact ISC2 objective in internal metadata, and explain why every choice is right or wrong. Use entirely original stems, distractors, and explanations.
2. Start with the largest CISSP domain (Security and Risk Management) but sample all eight domains according to the outline weights. The available textbook is foundational and does **not** cover all eight domains at CISSP depth; use additional authoritative sources before scaling the bank.
3. Review date-sensitive details against current publisher pages. In particular, NIST 800-53 Release 5.2.0 is newer than its downloaded base PDF, while NIST 800-61 Rev. 3 and 800-63 Rev. 4 supersede older references.
4. Keep ISC2 branding and the actual exam outline separate from original practice content. Do not ingest commercial CISSP study guides or exam dumps without a proper license.
