## Behavior

- For high-alert and look-alike/sound-alike medications, apply the pharmacy's error-prevention controls, such as separating inventory, using Tall Man lettering or barcode systems, and avoiding error-prone abbreviations.
- Keep the correct patient linked to the medication order during processing.

## Defaults or baseline

- PTCB lists these as examples of error-prevention strategies; local procedures determine how the controls are applied in a particular pharmacy.

## Gotchas

- A single safeguard is not evidence that an order is correct; the objective names multiple strategies and includes matching the medication order to the correct patient.

## Verify

- Review the pharmacy's current workflow for high-alert and look-alike/sound-alike products and confirm which specified controls are used at the relevant processing step.

## Applies to

- PTCE 2026 Patient Safety and Quality Assurance, objectives 3.1 and 3.2.
