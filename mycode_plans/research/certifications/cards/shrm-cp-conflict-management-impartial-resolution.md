## Behavior

- In a workplace conflict, identify the underlying causes and areas of common interest, then facilitate a respectful, impartial discussion aimed at a workable outcome.
- Refer the matter to a higher level when the situation warrants escalation.

## Defaults or baseline

- The BASK describes conflict management as resolving conflicts by identifying common interests; it lists accommodation, collaboration, compromise, competition, and avoidance as possible strategies.

## Gotchas

- Taking one party's side before understanding the causes can undermine impartiality and leave the underlying conflict unresolved.

- Compare the response with the BASK's all-HR-professional proficiency indicators for Conflict Management and confirm it addresses the cause, impartial facilitation, and escalation when warranted.
