# Behavior

- When a previously identified risk occurs, treat it as an issue and work with relevant stakeholders on a resolution.
- Reassess impediments as work proceeds so blockers continue to receive attention.

# Gotchas

- Leaving an occurred risk in the risk register as a future possibility can delay action on a current issue.

- Compare the scenario to Business Environment Task 4 in PMI's July 2026 outline, especially its risk-to-issue and stakeholder-resolution enablers.
