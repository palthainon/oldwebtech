## Behavior

- When processing reveals a listed concern such as an allergy, drug interaction, adverse drug event, misuse concern, or need for therapeutic substitution, route the issue for pharmacist intervention rather than making a clinical decision independently.
- Follow the pharmacy's approved workflow and applicable state technician scope requirements.

## Defaults or baseline

- The PTCE knowledge reference identifies the listed concerns as issues requiring pharmacist intervention.

## Gotchas

- Treating an intervention trigger as a routine data-entry correction can delay pharmacist review of a patient-safety concern.
- Technician scope and workflow requirements vary by state; this card does not establish a universal scope rule.

## Verify

- Confirm the concern matches the current pharmacy escalation workflow and the relevant state board requirements; refer clinical judgment to the pharmacist.

## Applies to

- PTCE 2026 Patient Safety and Quality Assurance, objective 3.3.
